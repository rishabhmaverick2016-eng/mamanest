## 🌟 Why MamaNest Exists

Taking care of twin toddlers in a joint family (with in-laws, brother, sister-in-law, and little Jainam) is doing three full-time jobs simultaneously. When babies cry, scream, throw things, or fight, a mother's nervous system naturally enters "fight-or-flight" (overstimulation / touched out / social anxiety under family scrutiny). In the heat of the moment, she snaps, blames them, or wants to escape—and immediately feels heavy mom-guilt.

**MamaNest** is her personal, non-judgmental pocket sanctuary that reminds her:
1. **She is NOT failing** — Raising twins in a joint home is an Olympic feat of endurance.
2. **Toddler Brain Reality** — Before age 3, logic does NOT work. Crying and throwing are brain development, not defiance.
3. **Pampering & Co-Regulation** — Why telling them to "stop crying" fails, why carrying them works like magic, and 6 gentle ways to soothe them.
4. **🏡 Joint Family Haven & 3-Toddler Harmony** — Handling toy sharing and comparisons between Khush, Khwaish, and cousin Jainam.
5. **Daily Twin Care Tracker** — Morning vitamins, D3 drops, iron, dental care, and sick-day medication logs with safety timers.
6. **🌬️ Nebulizer Fun Station** — Interactive bubble-popping game to keep Khush & Khwaish happily distracted while wearing the nebulizer mask for colds.
7. **🍲 What to Make for Them** — 5-minute easy meals, soft finger foods, and cold-soothing comfort foods with a "Pick for Me" button when tired.
8. **❤️ The Love Vault** — A celebration of how tiny and blessed Khush and Khwaish are (1 year, 9 months old), with a special love note from Khushal explaining *Restraint Collapse*.

## 🚀 How to Run

### Option 1: Double Click (Windows)
Double-click `START_APP.bat` in the `MamaNest` folder.

### Option 2: Command Line
```bash
node server.js
```

The app will open automatically at:
- **On this computer:** `http://localhost:3456`
- **On Wife's Phone via Wi-Fi:** `http://<Your-Local-IP>:3456` (shown in terminal)

---

## 📱 How Wife Can Install on Her Phone (PWA)
1. Ensure both computer and phone are connected to the same home Wi-Fi.
2. Open the phone's browser (Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android) and enter the Wi-Fi link.
3. **iPhone (Safari):** Tap the Share button (square with arrow) ➡️ **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. **Android (Chrome):** Tap the three dots (⋮) ➡️ **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
5. MamaNest will now sit on her home screen just like an App Store / Play Store app!
